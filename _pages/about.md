## Research Focus

My current work asks how visual object information is represented across cortical
and subcortical systems, especially when perception is linked to grasping and
other visually guided actions. I use MRI, eye tracking, electrophysiology, and
computational analysis to study how the visual system organizes information
about objects, scenes, and behaviorally relevant cues.

Previously, I worked at the Brain Computing Laboratory at the University of
Tehran and IPM on eye-tracking systems, visual imagery decoding from EEG,
sentence processing in autism, and central versus peripheral visual
representations in electrophysiological recordings.
